# Find community

**Community first.** Build for people you can reach this week.

## Questions

- Who already hacks together a workaround?
- Where do they post? (subreddit, Slack, HN, industry forum)
- Can you contribute value there **before** selling?

## Research

- Platform `docs/data/fetched/painpoints/reddit_*.csv`, `hn_*.csv` — **research** skill
- 10 specific handles or companies to watch

## Output

- Community map (place → why relevant)
- Contribution plan (2 weeks, no pitch)
- Validation interview list (**user-interview**)

## Red flag

- No identifiable gathering place → narrow niche or wrong problem
