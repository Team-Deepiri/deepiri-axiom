# User interview

## Before

- Hypothesis (problem, segment)
- 5–8 open questions, no leading
- Jobs-to-be-done: what were you trying to accomplish?

## During

- Record themes, not pitch
- Ask for current workaround and last time problem hurt

## After

- Affinity map: pains, quotes, frequency
- Surprises vs hypothesis

## Output

Interview summary + **validated / invalidated** per assumption → **validate-idea**
