# Axiom Adr Writing

Use for any decision that would be expensive to reverse or non-obvious to a future reader.

## Shape
1. **Context** — what forced this decision, what constraints applied.
2. **Decision** — the choice made, stated plainly.
3. **Consequences** — what this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what was explicitly given up.

Write the ADR at decision time, not months later from memory — the tradeoffs considered and rejected are the valuable part, not just the final choice.
