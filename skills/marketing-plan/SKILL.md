# Marketing plan

**Sales before marketing at scale.** Marketing amplifies what already sells.

## Preconditions

- Paying customers exist (**first-customers**)
- Repeatable manual or product delivery (**processize**)

## Plan elements

1. **One channel** — master before adding (blog, newsletter, video, podcast guest)
2. **Teaching** — share how you solved the problem for real users
3. **Proof** — screenshots, metrics, quotes (permissioned)
4. **Cadence** — sustainable (weekly > daily burnout)

## Avoid

- Ads before organic conversion works
- Brand spend before positioning is one sentence clear

## Output

- 90-day content calendar (12 pieces)
- CTA per piece (trial, call, waitlist)
- Success metric (signups, demos, not vanity likes)
