# Validate idea

**Validation = selling, not building.** Prove pain and payment before platform work.

## Steps

1. **Problem** — Who exactly? Current workaround? Hair-on-fire or mild?
2. **Manual first** — Can you deliver value by hand this week?
3. **Payment** — Will 3+ people pay (even $1)?
4. **Weekend test** — Can the first wedge ship in a weekend?

## Red flags

- No existing workaround; can't name 10 people; friends-only enthusiasm; must educate market they have the problem.

## Green flags

- Paying for inferior solutions; you delivered manually and they loved it; community already complaining; you scratch your own itch.

## Deepiri

- Check `deepiri-dataset-processor` output and `docs/` for existing signal
- If validated → **mvp** / **processize**; if platform wedge → **deepiri-axiom** + **axiom-architect**
