# Processize

**Processize before productize.** Gumroad-style: manual fulfillment first, software second.

## Deliverables

1. One sentence: what the product does **manually**
2. **Magic piece of paper** — trigger, numbered steps, tools, minutes/customer, handoff
3. Three named people to serve this week
4. Initial price
5. **First automation candidate** (later — not now)

## Strip the idea

- One job, one outcome
- Before/after for the customer
- Zero-tech version: phone + spreadsheet + email

## Ready to productize when

- 10+ paying manual deliveries
- Steps stable for weeks
- Delivery time dominates acquisition time

## Deepiri handoff

- Automate proven steps → **ship** + right repo (**deepiri-web-frontend** UX vs **diri-cyrex** agents vs **diri-helox** training)
