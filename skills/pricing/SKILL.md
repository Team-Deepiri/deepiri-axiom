# Pricing

**Charge something.** Free ≠ signal; $1 changes behavior.

## Models

- **Cost-based** — costs + margin (services, hardware)
- **Value-based** — willingness to pay (software, AI features)

## Initial price questions

1. Variable cost per customer?
2. What do alternatives charge?
3. No-brainer price for ideal customer?
4. Profitable from customer #1?

## Math

- Monthly need ÷ price = customers required
- 1 customer/business day → months to sustainability

## Principles

- Start low, raise with proof
- Tiers later (economy / business / first class)
- Free trial OK with clear paid path — not permanent free tier as strategy

## Output

Model, price + rationale, future tiers sketch, customer count target, when to revisit.
